**Iris Evangeline Lucas** is our first child, and she's due in June 2027. This is where we're keeping her story, starting before she arrives and adding to it as she grows.

## Her name

**Iris** comes from the Greek word for *rainbow*. In Greek myth, Iris was the messenger who carried word between the heavens and the earth along a rainbow.

**Evangeline** comes from the Greek *euangelion*, meaning *good news*.

Together, her name means something like *a rainbow carrying good news*. We think that fits her.
